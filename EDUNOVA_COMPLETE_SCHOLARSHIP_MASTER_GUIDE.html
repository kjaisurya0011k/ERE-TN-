👷 Chapter 2: Tamil Nadu Welfare Board Scholarships & Official Portals

1. TN Construction Workers Welfare Board (TNCWWB)

Eligibility: Either parent registered in TN Construction Workers Welfare Board.

Benefits: Arts/Science: ₹1,500 – ₹3,000/yr | Professional (B.E./MBBS/Agri): ₹4,000 – ₹6,000/yr (Hostel assistance up to ₹12,000/yr).

Official Website: https://tnuwwb.tn.gov.in

Direct Scheme Portal: https://tnuwwb.tn.gov.in/schemes | Helpline: 044-24335111

2. TN Unorganized Workers Welfare Boards (18 Notified Boards)

Eligibility: Children of registered auto drivers, tailors, handloom weavers, and manual laborers.

Benefits: Annual educational stipends & hostel allowance direct via DBT.

Official Application Portal: https://www.tnesevai.tn.gov.in

3. TN Labour Welfare Board (TNLWB - Organized Sector)

Eligibility: Children of factory/IT/shop workers contributing to Labour Welfare Fund (Basic salary ≤ ₹25,000/month).

Benefits: Engineering/Medicine: ₹12,000 – ₹50,000/yr | Arts/Science: ₹3,000 – ₹10,000/yr + Book Allowance.

Official Website: https://labour.tn.gov.in

Scheme Details: https://tils.tn.gov.in/schemes

🏛️ Chapter 3: Tamil Nadu State Government 100% Free Schemes & Official Websites

1. 7.5% Government School Quota (100% Full Free Education)

Eligibility: 6th to 12th studied in TN Government Schools and admitted via counseling (TNEA, NEET, TNAU, TANUVAS, TNDALU).

Benefits: 100% Tuition fees, Hostel accommodation, Mess food, and University Exam fees fully paid by TN Govt.

Counseling Portal: https://www.tneaonline.org | Department: tn.gov.in/department/11

2. Tamil Nadu First Graduate Scholarship (முதல் தலைமுறை பட்டதாரி)

Eligibility: First graduate in the entire family admitted via single-window counseling.

Benefits: ₹25,000 to ₹27,500/year direct tuition fee waiver.

Certificate Portal: https://www.tnesevai.tn.gov.in

3. Pudhumai Penn (Girls) & Tamil Pudhalvan (Boys) Schemes

Eligibility: 6th to 12th studied in Govt / Govt-Aided (Tamil Medium) Schools pursuing regular UG/Diploma.

Benefits: ₹1,000 per month (₹12,000/year) deposited directly to student bank account.

Official Portals: pudhumaipenn.tn.gov.in | tamilpudhalvan.tn.gov.in

4. Post-Matric 100% Free Education (SC / ST / SCA / SCC)

Eligibility: SC/ST/SCA/SCC students with family annual income < ₹2.5 Lakhs.

Benefits: 100% Full Tuition Fee Waiver in Govt & Self-Financing colleges + Monthly Maintenance Allowance.

Official Portal: https://escholarship.tn.gov.in
