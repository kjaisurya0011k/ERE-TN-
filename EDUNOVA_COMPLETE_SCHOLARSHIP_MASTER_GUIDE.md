# Engineering Cut-Off Formula & Overview

For admission into Tamil Nadu Engineering Colleges (TNEA) and institutional scholarships, the cut-off is calculated out of **200 Marks**:

$$\text{Engineering Cut-Off (200)} = \text{Mathematics (100)} + \left(\frac{\text{Physics (100)}}{2}\right) + \left(\frac{\text{Chemistry (100)}}{2}\right)$$

# Chapter 2: Tamil Nadu Welfare Board Scholarships & Official Portals

### 1. Tamil Nadu Construction Workers Welfare Board (TNCWWB)
* **Eligibility:** Either father or mother must be an active registered member in the TN Construction Workers Welfare Board.
* **Benefits:** Arts & Science: ₹1,500 – ₹3,000/yr | Professional (B.E./MBBS/Agri/Law): ₹4,000 – ₹6,000/yr (Hostel allowance up to ₹12,000/yr).
* **Official Website:** [https://tnuwwb.tn.gov.in](https://tnuwwb.tn.gov.in)
* **Direct Scheme Portal:** [https://tnuwwb.tn.gov.in/schemes](https://tnuwwb.tn.gov.in/schemes)
* **Helpline:** `044-24335111`

### 2. Tamil Nadu Unorganized Workers Welfare Boards (18 Notified Craft Boards)
* **Eligibility:** Children of registered auto drivers, tailors, handloom weavers, and manual laborers.
* **Benefits:** Annual educational stipend & hostel aid directly credited via DBT.
* **Official Website:** [https://tnuwwb.tn.gov.in](https://tnuwwb.tn.gov.in)
* **Online Application Portal (e-Sevai):** [https://www.tnesevai.tn.gov.in](https://www.tnesevai.tn.gov.in)

### 3. Tamil Nadu Labour Welfare Board (TNLWB - Organized Sector)
* **Eligibility:** Children of workers contributing to the Tamil Nadu Labour Welfare Fund (Monthly basic salary $\le$ ₹25,000).
* **Benefits:** Engineering/Medicine: ₹12,000 – ₹50,000/yr | Arts/Science: ₹3,000 – ₹10,000/yr + Book Allowance.
* **Official Website:** [https://labour.tn.gov.in](https://labour.tn.gov.in)
* **Application / Schemes Portal:** [https://tils.tn.gov.in/schemes](https://tils.tn.gov.in/schemes) & [https://labour.tn.gov.in/labourwelfare/](https://labour.tn.gov.in/labourwelfare/)

# Chapter 3: Tamil Nadu State Government 100% Free Schemes & Official Websites

### 1. 7.5% Government School Quota (100% Full Free Education)
* **Eligibility:** Students who studied continuously from **Class 6 to 12 in Tamil Nadu Government Schools** admitted via counseling (TNEA, NEET, TNAU, TANUVAS, TNJFU, TNDALU).
* **Benefits:** **100% Full Free Education** $\rightarrow$ Complete Tuition, Special fees, Hostel accommodation, Mess food, and University Exam fees paid by TN Government.
* **Official Information:** [https://www.tn.gov.in/department/11](https://www.tn.gov.in/department/11)
* **Counseling Application Portal:** [https://www.tneaonline.org](https://www.tneaonline.org) & [https://tnmedicalselection.net](https://tnmedicalselection.net)

### 2. Tamil Nadu First Graduate Scholarship (முதல் தலைமுறை பட்டதாரி)
* **Eligibility:** The student must be the first person in their family to pursue graduation; admitted via single-window counseling.
* **Benefits:** Direct tuition fee waiver of **₹25,000/year** (Non-Accredited courses) or **₹27,500/year** (Accredited courses).
* **Certificate Portal (e-Sevai):** [https://www.tnesevai.tn.gov.in](https://www.tnesevai.tn.gov.in)
* **Counseling Verification:** [https://www.tneaonline.org](https://www.tneaonline.org)

### 3. Pudhumai Penn Scheme (Moovalur Ramamirtham Ammaiyar Scheme - Girls)
* **Eligibility:** Girl students who studied from Class 6 to 12 in Tamil Nadu Government Schools pursuing any regular UG Degree, Diploma, or ITI.
* **Benefits:** **₹1,000 every month** (₹12,000/year) directly transferred into student bank account.
* **Official Portal:** [https://pudhumaipenn.tn.gov.in](https://pudhumaipenn.tn.gov.in)
* **Department Website:** [https://www.tn.gov.in/department/30](https://www.tn.gov.in/department/30)

### 4. Tamil Pudhalvan Scheme (Boys)
* **Eligibility:** Male students who studied from Class 6 to 12 in Tamil Nadu Government / Govt-Aided (Tamil Medium) Schools joining regular UG degree/diploma courses.
* **Benefits:** **₹1,000 every month** (₹12,000/year) direct monthly allowance.
* **Official Website:** [https://tamilpudhalvan.tn.gov.in](https://tamilpudhalvan.tn.gov.in)

### 5. Post-Matric Scholarship for SC / ST / SCA / SCC (Converted Christians)
* **Eligibility:** SC, ST, SCA, and SCC students with annual family income $< ₹2,50,000$.
* **Benefits:** **100% Full Tuition Fee Waiver** in Govt and Self-Financing colleges + Monthly Maintenance Allowance.
* **Official Portal:** [https://escholarship.tn.gov.in](https://escholarship.tn.gov.in)
* **Department Website:** [https://adw.tn.gov.in](https://adw.tn.gov.in)

### 6. Post-Matric Scholarship for BC / MBC / DNC
* **Eligibility:** BC, MBC, and DNC students with family annual income $< ₹2,50,000$ studying in regular UG/Professional courses.
* **Benefits:** Tuition fee reimbursement in Govt/Govt-Aided colleges + Non-refundable fees and hostel allowance.
* **Official Portal:** [https://bcmbcmw.tn.gov.in](https://bcmbcmw.tn.gov.in)

# Chapter 5: Central Government & AICTE Scholarships (NSP Portal)

### 1. Central Sector Scheme of Scholarship (PM-USP / CSSS)
* **Eligibility:** Scored above **80th percentile** in 12th Board Examinations; Family income $< ₹4,50,000$/year.
* **Benefits:** **₹12,000/year** (Years 1 to 3 of UG) and **₹20,000/year** (Years 4 & 5).
* **Official Information:** [https://www.education.gov.in/scholarship-schemes](https://www.education.gov.in/scholarship-schemes)
* **Application Portal (NSP):** [https://scholarships.gov.in](https://scholarships.gov.in)

### 2. AICTE Pragati Scholarship for Girl Students
* **Eligibility:** Up to 2 girl children per family admitted to 1st year of AICTE-approved Degree / Diploma (Engineering / Technology / Pharmacy); Income $< ₹8,00,000$/year.
* **Benefits:** **₹50,000 per year** for all 4 years of study towards tuition fees, laptop, and academic expenses.
* **Official Information:** [https://www.aicte-india.org/schemes/students-development-schemes/Pragati](https://www.aicte-india.org/schemes/students-development-schemes/Pragati)
* **Application Portal (NSP):** [https://scholarships.gov.in](https://scholarships.gov.in)

### 3. AICTE Saksham & Swanath Scholarships
* **Eligibility:**
  * *Saksham:* Specially-abled students (Disability $\ge 40\%$) in technical courses.
  * *Swanath:* Orphaned students or children whose parents died due to COVID-19 / Armed Forces martyrs.
* **Benefits:** **₹50,000 per year** for the full duration of the degree.
* **Official Information:** [https://www.aicte-india.org/schemes/students-development-schemes](https://www.aicte-india.org/schemes/students-development-schemes)
* **Application Portal (NSP):** [https://scholarships.gov.in](https://scholarships.gov.in)

### 4. Central Post-Matric Scholarship for Minorities
* **Eligibility:** Muslim, Christian, Sikh, Buddhist, Jain, Parsi students with $\ge 50\%$ in 12th; Family income $< ₹2,00,000$/year.
* **Benefits:** Full tuition fee reimbursement + annual maintenance allowance.
* **Official Information:** [https://www.minorityaffairs.gov.in](https://www.minorityaffairs.gov.in)
* **Application Portal (NSP):** [https://scholarships.gov.in](https://scholarships.gov.in)

# Chapter 7: Essential Documents Checklist & Application Action Plan

Keep these documents scanned and ready:
1. **10th & 12th Standard Original Marksheets**
2. **Transfer Certificate (TC) & Conduct Certificate**
3. **Permanent Community Certificate** (BC / BCM / MBC / DNC / SC / SCA / ST)
4. **Income Certificate** (Recent from e-Sevai, issued within the last 6 months)
5. **Nativity Certificate & Residence Proof**
6. **First Graduate Certificate & Joint Declaration** (if eligible)
7. **Parent's Welfare Board Membership Passbook** (if applying under Welfare Board)
8. **Student's Own Bank Account Passbook** (Aadhaar Seeded / DBT Enabled)
9. **Aadhaar Card** (Linked with student's mobile number)
10. **Special Category Proof** (7.5% Govt School 6th-12th Bonafide Certificate, Disability Certificate for PwD, Sports Certificates)
