# Pillar 2: All Grassroots NGO Foundations & Philanthropic Trusts (100% Free Higher Education)

Grassroots NGO Foundations in Tamil Nadu provide **100% full-ride education**, free boarding/mess, laptops, English communication training, and career mentorship for students from economically challenged backgrounds.

### 1. Agni Siragukal Foundation (அக்னிச் சிறகுகள் அறக்கட்டளை)
* **Managing Trust:** Agni Siragugal Educational & Social Welfare Trust (Inspired by Dr. APJ Abdul Kalam's Vision 2020)
* **Core Mission:** Identifying underprivileged, orphan, single-parent, and rural government school students across Tamil Nadu and sponsoring 100% higher education fees.
* **Benefits:**
  * **100% College Tuition Fee Payment** directly to college.
  * **Free College Study Materials, Books, and Examination Fees.**
  * **Personality Development & Career Coaching Mentorship.**
* **Eligibility:**
  * Class 12 Passed students with $\ge 75\%$ marks (Government / Govt-Aided Schools given top preference).
  * Annual family income $< ₹1,50,000$ (Preference for single-parent/orphans).
* **Official Contacts & Verification:** Agni Siragukal Trust, Tamil Nadu | Physical verification via local coordinators.

### 2. Agaram Foundation ("Vidhai" Program)
* **Founder / Trust:** Suriya Sivakumar & Agaram Foundation
* **Benefits:** **100% Full-Ride Sponsorship** (Tuition Fees + Shared Hostel + Mess Food + English Fluency Training + Life Skills Mentoring).
* **Eligibility:** Rural government school students with high 12th marks, first-generation graduates, orphan/single-parent children.
* **Process:** Application immediately after 12th results $\rightarrow$ Detailed home verification by Agaram volunteers $\rightarrow$ Final Interview $\rightarrow$ College Allotment.
* **Official Portal:** [https://agaram.in](https://agaram.in)

### 3. Maatram Foundation
* **Founder / Trust:** Sujith Kumar & Maatram Educational Trust
* **Benefits:** **100% Zero-Fee Higher Education** (Zero Tuition, Zero Hostel, Zero Food, Zero Bus Fees across 50+ partnered top colleges in Tamil Nadu).
* **Eligibility:** Top marks in 12th Board Exams, daily wage labor parents, orphan/destitute background, annual family income $< ₹1,00,000$.
* **Official Portal:** [https://maatramfoundation.com](https://maatramfoundation.com)

### 4. Anandam Youth Foundation
* **Founder / Trust:** Bhageerathi & Anandam Educational Trust
* **Benefits:** **100% College Tuition + Free Hostel + Free Food + Free Laptop + Continuous Mentorship.**

### 5. Team Everest NGO ("I am the Future" Scholarship)

# Pillar 4: All ID Card & Welfare Board Related Scholarships (Kattumana Attai, 18 Welfare Boards, etc.)

In Tamil Nadu, students whose parents possess government-issued labor, community, or special identity cards are entitled to statutory educational scholarships, cash incentives, and hostel allowances.

---

### 💳 1. Tamil Nadu Construction Workers Welfare Card (கட்டுமான நல வாரிய அட்டை - TNCWWB / Kattumana Attai)
* **Governing Body:** Tamil Nadu Construction Workers Welfare Board (தமிழ்நாடு கட்டுமானத் தொழிலாளர்கள் நல வாரியம்).
* **Card Name:** தமிழ்நாடு கட்டுமானத் தொழிலாளர் அடையாள அட்டை (TNCWWB Passbook / Smart Card).
* **Official Online Portal:** [https://tnuwwb.tn.gov.in](https://tnuwwb.tn.gov.in) & [https://www.tnesevai.tn.gov.in](https://www.tnesevai.tn.gov.in)
* **Eligibility:** Either father or mother must be an active registered member with valid renewal in the board.

#### 📊 Mandatory Benefit Disbursement Schedule (Kattumana Attai):
| Academic Course Level | Day Scholar Grant (Per Year) | Hosteller Grant (Per Year) | Additional Board Incentives |
|---|---|---|---|
| **10th Standard Passed Incentive** | ₹1,000 (One-time) | — | Cash award for scoring $\ge 400/500$ |
| **12th Standard Passed Incentive** | ₹1,500 (One-time) | — | Cash award for scoring $\ge 500/600$ |
| **ITI / Certificate Courses** | ₹1,500 / year | ₹2,000 / year | Free tool kit assistance |
| **Polytechnic Diploma Courses** | ₹2,000 / year | ₹3,000 / year | Practical training allowance |
| **General UG Degrees (B.A., B.Sc., B.Com, BCA, BBA)** | ₹2,500 – ₹3,000 / year | ₹4,000 – ₹5,000 / year | Annual book grant |
| **Professional UG (B.E., B.Tech, MBBS, BDS, B.Sc. Agri, B.V.Sc, Law)** | ₹4,000 – ₹6,000 / year | **₹12,000 / year** (Dedicated Hostel Grant) | Priority hostel allotment |
| **Postgraduate Degrees (M.A., M.Sc., M.Com, MCA, MBA)** | ₹4,000 / year | ₹6,000 / year | Project assistance grant |
| **Professional PG (M.E., M.Tech, M.D., M.S., M.L.)** | ₹6,000 / year | **₹12,000 / year** | Dissertation grant |

* **Required Verification Documents:**
  1. Parent's Original Welfare Board Membership Card / Smart Card (உறுப்பினர் அட்டை).
  2. Active Renewal Seal / Slip for current year.
  3. Student 10th / 12th Marksheet.
  4. Bonafide Certificate with Study Fees from College Principal / Head of Institution.
  5. If staying in hostel: Hostel Warden Bonafide Certificate (விடுதி சான்றிதழ்).
  6. Student Aadhaar Card & Bank Passbook (DBT Linked).

# Comprehensive Document Verification & Application Workflow Checklist

To ensure seamless approval across College Trusts, NGOs, and Government Welfare Boards, maintain the following digital and physical document binder:

1. **Aadhaar - Bank Account Seeding:** Ensure the student's personal bank account has active NPCI Aadhaar seeding for Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) credit.
2. **e-Sevai Certificates:** Community, Income, Nativity, and First Graduate certificates must carry valid 16-digit e-Sevai barcode verification numbers.
3. **Welfare Board Passbook:** For Kattumana Attai & 18 Craft Boards, ensure the **annual renewal stamp/receipt** for the current financial year is verified before submitting the application.
